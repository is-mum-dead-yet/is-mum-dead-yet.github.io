✳ THE DEVELOPMENT DIARY / PRELUDE

It started
with a lamp.

Sometimes the smallest things make the biggest difference. ♡

Before the beacons, sensors and software, there was a little button on Mum's trolley.

Sometimes the simplest things give you the biggest ideas.

I'd been thinking about ways to make a few everyday things a little easier for Mum. She has a trolley that she walks around with, and she prefers having a small lamp on in the front room rather than switching on all the main lights.

So, while I was visiting, I put a little Tapo smart button on her trolley and connected it to the Tapo smart plug controlling that lamp.

The idea was wonderfully straightforward: when she wanted a bit of light, she could press the button. No need to walk across the room to find a switch.

♡

One button. One lamp.
And Mum just got it.

And that's exactly what happened. I showed her how it worked, and she understood it straight away.

Better still, she's never forgotten. She uses that little button all the time.

She's even found her own way of using it. When she's coming out of her bedroom, she'll turn the lamp on as she walks out, so there's some light ahead of her. She doesn't have to make her way all the way across the hallway and into the front room just to switch a light on.

That might sound like a tiny improvement. But it's something she chose to use, without reminders or a complicated new routine. The technology fitted into her life instead of asking her to fit her life around it.

And that got me thinking…

If one inexpensive button and a smart plug could make something easier, what other little problems could we solve?

Could a few ordinary gadgets help us understand when there's been activity around the house, without Mum having to wear something, learn an app or feel like she's being watched?

I didn't have all the answers. I still don't. But that little lamp made me curious enough to start experimenting.

And that's what this diary is about: the ideas, the tests, the surprises, and the times something works far better than you expected.

It all started with a lamp.
