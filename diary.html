✳ NOTES FROM THE EXPERIMENT

The development diary.

Small discoveries. Big questions. Lots of tea. ♡

This diary is intended to give you a non-technical overview of the different stages of development, with technical detail where it's useful to explain what we're doing and why.

It's the story of building HomePulse around Mum's real life: what we've tried, what worked, what didn't, and what we learned. You won't need to be a developer to follow along.

A LITTLE CONTEXT

Not a polished launch story.
A real experiment.

HomePulse is a work in progress. We'll be open about uncertainty, rough edges and ideas that haven't been proven. It's a personal home-awareness project, not a medical monitor or emergency service. A sensor can offer a clue, never a guarantee that somebody is safe or well.
